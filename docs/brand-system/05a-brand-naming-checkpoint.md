# Phase 5A — Brand Naming Checkpoint

## Status

**Paused — not finalized.**

Naming exploration is intentionally stopped at this checkpoint to prevent further delay to the V2 brand-system build. The product may continue into symbol, app-icon, color, typography, and broader visual-system development without a final public brand name.

The final name must be selected before the final wordmark, legal filing, store listing, launch assets, and production brand handoff are locked.

## Current Legal-Clearance Finalists

The following names are retained as the strongest current candidates after preliminary collision and legal-risk screening:

1. **ATURVA** — current leading candidate; strongest preliminary distinctiveness / clearance profile.
2. **HAURVA** — strong whole-health / longevity meaning; requires final EU/Italy trade-name and trademark clearance.
3. **HIRKAN** — strong Iranian heritage / nature / longevity territory; requires deeper health/pharma similarity clearance.

These names are **not final selections** and should not be treated as approved production names.

## Naming Decision Rule

Naming should resume only when one of the following becomes true:

- the visual identity is mature enough to test the finalists in real brand contexts;
- legal counsel completes formal clearance;
- a materially stronger candidate appears without reopening broad, open-ended naming exploration.

## Required Final Legal Clearance

Before selecting a final name, run exact and similarity searches across the intended markets and relevant Nice classes, especially:

- Class 9 — apps, software, wearables where applicable
- Class 41 — fitness, coaching, education, training
- Class 42 — SaaS, AI, software services
- Class 44 — health, wellness, medical-related services
- Class 10 — only if medical devices become part of the product scope

Priority registries/jurisdictions include USPTO, EUIPO/TMview, WIPO Madrid Monitor, UKIPO, and TÜRKPATENT.

## Naming Guardrails Retained

The final master brand should remain compatible with the approved brand strategy:

- globally pronounceable and visually strong in Latin script;
- non-generic and non-literal in health/fitness/AI;
- extensible beyond workouts into a broader Personal Health Intelligence Platform;
- compatible with Premium Health Technology positioning;
- capable of carrying Future Self, Human Intelligence, Adaptive Intelligence, discipline, longevity, and whole-person health without requiring all of those ideas to be literal in the name;
- distinctive enough to support trademark ownership and searchability;
- not dependent on trend-driven AI naming conventions.

## Next Step

Proceed to **Phase 5B — Logo & Symbol System** using a **name-agnostic symbol-first process**. The final wordmark is deferred until naming is resolved.
