# 05a — Brand Naming Checkpoint

## Final name

**RUNPUY**

Always typeset in uppercase in the custom logo. In prose, RUNPUY is preferred; `Runpuy` may be used only where platform capitalization is constrained.

## Meaning

The name combines forward movement with the Persian concept of *puyidan*—to move, seek and progress. It should be explained as a brand story, not translated literally in product copy.

## Tagline

**KEEP PUYING**

The tagline remains English-only. Do not force a Persian equivalent. Use it as a campaign or brand-signoff line, not on every interface screen.

## Handles

Preferred: `runpuy`; fallback: `runpuyofficial`. Existing platform availability must be verified at registration time.
