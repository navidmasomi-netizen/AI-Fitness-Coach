# 09 — Iconography

## Precision Motion

Approved icon language: 24 px grid, 1.8 px stroke, round line caps and joins, with controlled angular accents. Icons must remain recognizable at 20, 24 and 32 px.

Primary navigation: Home, Workout, Progress, AI Coach and Profile. Health/performance: Heart, Sleep, Recovery, Readiness, Steps, Calories and Timer.

## Usage

- Default icons are single-color.
- Active navigation may use Deep Green on Pale Mint.
- Semantic color is allowed only when the icon communicates a semantic health state.
- Always pair unfamiliar health icons with labels.
- Do not mix filled third-party icons with Precision Motion within one navigation set.

Standalone SVG masters are in `assets/brand/05-iconography/`.
