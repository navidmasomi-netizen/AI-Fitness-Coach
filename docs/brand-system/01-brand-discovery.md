# 01 — Brand Discovery

## Product idea

RUNPUY turns fragmented fitness and health signals into understandable, personal next steps. It is an AI fitness coach, but the brand promise extends beyond workouts: it helps people read their body, choose an appropriate action, and keep progressing.

## Audience

- People who want structured improvement without information overload
- Active beginners and intermediate users who need consistency
- Data-aware users who value credible health context
- Multilingual users, including English and Persian readers

## Core need

“Tell me what my health and performance data means, and what I should do today.”

## Desired perception

Intelligent, trustworthy, progressive, premium, human and calm. Avoid aggressive bodybuilding clichés, neon gaming aesthetics, generic wellness softness and medical coldness.
