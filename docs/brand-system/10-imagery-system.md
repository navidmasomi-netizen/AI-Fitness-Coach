# 10 — Imagery System

## Mix

- 60% Health Intelligence: data-aware training, recovery and coaching context
- 25% Human Performance: real movement, effort and form
- 15% Everyday Momentum: walking, routines and attainable progress

## Art direction

Prefer natural light, authentic environments, inclusive subjects and clear movement. Crop with room for interface/data overlays. Skin tones must remain natural; Emerald may appear as a UI overlay but should not tint people.

Avoid extreme transformations, staged medical scenarios, generic stock-smile imagery, excessive muscle isolation, unsafe exercise form and unlicensed images.

No stock photography is bundled in this release. Image selection requires documented licensing and model releases.
