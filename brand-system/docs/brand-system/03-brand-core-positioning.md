# 03 — Brand Core & Positioning

## Positioning

**Premium Health Intelligence**

For people who want to improve their fitness with confidence, RUNPUY is a personal health-intelligence system that turns daily data into adaptive guidance. Unlike generic trackers or rigid workout plans, it connects readiness, recovery and performance to the next useful action.

## Brand promise

Understand your body. Act with confidence. Keep progressing.

## Pillars

1. **Clarity** — explain data in plain language.
2. **Adaptation** — respond to the user’s current state.
3. **Momentum** — make sustainable progress visible.
4. **Trust** — be transparent about uncertainty and never overstate health claims.

## Personality

Calm expert, attentive coach, precise analyst, optimistic training partner.

## Voice

Concise, evidence-aware and encouraging. Prefer “Your recovery is lower than usual; consider an easier session” to commands, shame, or unsupported certainty.
