# 11 — Product Foundations

## Structured Calm

Base spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32 px. Default page margin: 20 px. Minimum touch target: 44 × 44 px. Corner radii: 8 controls, 12 buttons, 16 cards and 18 insight surfaces.

## Responsive Calm motion

Durations: 180 ms fast feedback, 220 ms standard transition, 240 ms layered reveal. Motion should explain hierarchy or confirm action. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid continuous decorative animation.

## Data visualization

Lead with the takeaway, then supporting values and trend. Use direct labels where possible. Charts must remain understandable without relying on color alone and should expose accessible text equivalents.
