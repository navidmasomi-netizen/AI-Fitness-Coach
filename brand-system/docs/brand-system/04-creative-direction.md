# 04 — Creative Direction

## Approved direction

**Health Intelligence** combines structured calm with precise motion cues.

- Warm White surfaces keep the system open and human.
- Ink and Deep Navy establish trust and depth.
- Emerald identifies progress, readiness and primary action.
- Layered cards reveal insight from overview to detail.
- Photography shows attainable movement and real environments.
- Angular accents connect the custom R, icon system and motion language.

## Composition

Use clear hierarchy, generous negative space, strong alignment and one dominant message per frame. Data visualizations should highlight the decision, not merely the measurement.

## Avoid

Neon overload, decorative gradients without meaning, excessive glass effects, dense chart walls, bodybuilding stereotypes, medical imagery, hard-edged urgency and multiple competing calls to action.
