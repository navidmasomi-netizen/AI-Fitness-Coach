# 06 — Visual Identity

## Approved lockup

The primary signature is the custom RUNPUY wordmark followed by the compact framed R tile. The tile is aligned to the letter height and must not be enlarged as a separate emphasis.

## Hierarchy

1. `runpuy-primary-horizontal.svg` — default signature
2. `runpuy-primary-horizontal-reverse.svg` — dark backgrounds
3. `runpuy-wordmark-*` — narrow placements where the tile is redundant
4. `runpuy-symbol-*` — avatars, app icons and compact UI
5. `runpuy-with-tagline.svg` — controlled brand communications only

## Clear space and minimum size

Maintain clear space equal to at least half the R-tile width on every side. Minimum digital widths: 120 px for the horizontal signature and 24 px for the R mark. Below these sizes use the glyph or platform-specific icon.

## Color

Use Ink on Warm White, Warm White on Ink/Night Ink, or approved Emerald/Navy symbol variants. Do not recolor individual letters, outline the mark, add shadows, distort proportions, rotate, or detach/enlarge the right-hand tile inside the primary signature.
