# 02 — Competitive Brand Audit

## Market patterns

Fitness products often cluster into three visual territories: clinical dashboards, high-energy performance brands, or soft lifestyle wellness. Each communicates part of the RUNPUY promise, but none is complete alone.

## Opportunity

RUNPUY occupies **Health Intelligence**: the calm credibility of health products, the momentum of performance coaching, and the clarity of a premium digital tool.

## Differentiation rules

- Use Emerald as an action and progress signal, not as decorative “health green.”
- Use Deep Navy and Ink to create authority without defaulting to pure black.
- Prefer layered insight cards over dense dashboards.
- Keep claims specific, readable and supportive.
- Show real movement and daily progress rather than extreme physiques.

Competitive references are for category learning only; no third-party visual asset belongs in the production system.
